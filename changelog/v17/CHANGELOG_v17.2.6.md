# Beat UP! v17.2.6 — Song Info Panel Polish

## Focus
- improve the selected-song information hierarchy on the left side after the v17.2.5 16:9 rebalance;
- keep the browser, background, chart, gameplay, and progression baselines frozen.

## Changes
- Rebuilt Personal Best presentation from three spread-out mini columns into one compact vertical key/value block.
- Personal Best rows are now `SCORE`, `ACCURACY`, and `MAX COMBO`, with the value aligned on the right.
- `RANK` remains in the Personal Best header so the whole record reads as one compact unit.
- Added deliberate whitespace between chart progress and Personal Best without adding a panel, border, or container-heavy visual.
- Artist names now preserve natural capitalization instead of being forced to uppercase; unknown artists remain hidden.
- Slightly strengthened artist and chart metadata readability while keeping the title dominant.
- Personal Best judgement breakdown remains underneath the primary metrics, but is slightly quieter.
- v17.2.5 browser proportions, toolbar layout, row sizing, background treatment, and scrollbar are unchanged.

## Frozen systems
- No chart or timing changes.
- No gameplay changes.
- No progression/data logic changes.
- No song browser layout changes on the right side.
- No background asset or backdrop-rendering changes.
