# Beat UP! v18.5.0.1 — Scoreboard Layout Hotfix

## Fixed

- Increased the live scoreboard card height for seven-digit v18.5 scores.
- Separated the score and accuracy into distinct vertical rows.
- Prevented the ACCURACY caption and percentage from being covered by the
  large score digits at supported viewport sizes.

This hotfix does not change scoring, charts, note timing, backgrounds, or other
gameplay HUD elements.
